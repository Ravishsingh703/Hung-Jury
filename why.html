The film

12 Angry Men is one room, one afternoon of heat, and twelve men deciding whether a young man lives or dies. Eleven of them want to vote and go home. One of them doesn’t.

He never says the others are wrong. He just won’t sign off on something that serious without talking about it first. Then the film becomes the conversation.

What I took from it

  1. 01

    One person who isn’t sure is enough to start a conversation.

    Juror 8 never claims he’s right. He just refuses to vote without talking first. That’s the whole club, really.

  2. 02

    Changing your mind is the win, not the loss.

    Most rooms treat switching sides as losing. I wanted a table where it gets a toast, not a smirk.

  3. 03

    The loudest person in the room is usually defending something else.

    Most of us are arguing about something underneath the topic. Hung Jury is where you find out what yours is.

  4. 04

    Strangers can be honest in a way friends can’t.

    Twelve people who’d never met, made to think out loud together. No reputations to protect. Nobody to perform for.
