Before you sit down.

Four rules. We read them out loud at the start of every night, so nobody’s ever the new one.

Read them
1

Rule one

Go for the idea. Never the person.

Tear the argument apart. Leave the human in one piece.

2

Rule two

Go for the idea. Never the person.

Yes, again. Someone always forgets.

3

Rule three

Think out loud.

A raw thought of your own beats a perfect one you borrowed. No quotes. No “studies show”. Just you.

4

Rule four

Three ways to lose.

  • ×You hate the person who broke your argument.
  • ×Your mind never once crossed to the other side.
  • ×You came to win.

And one for the host

The host gets no protection.

If anyone genuinely changes their mind tonight, dessert’s on the host.
